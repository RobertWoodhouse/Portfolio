BreakfastLunch

Bacon
Sandwich

A high-protein, slow-release breakfast built to minimise glucose spikes. Every ingredient has been chosen to support insulin sensitivity without sacrificing flavour.

~575 kcal

Ingredients

IngredientAmountCalories
Seeded wholemeal medium-sliced bread3 slices (~36g each)255 kcal
Back bacon rashers3 rashers195 kcal
Kerrygold Spreadable Olive Oil (or block butter)~15g across 3 slices100 kcal
Extra virgin olive oil (for frying)½ tsp (~2.5g)20 kcal
Heinz Tomato Ketchup Zero~15g5 kcal

Method

1Fry the bacon

Heat a pan to medium heat (level 4 out of 6 on an electric hob). Add ½ tsp of extra virgin olive oil. Cook the 3 back bacon rashers until done to your preference, turning once. The bacon fat renders into the pan, so only a small amount of oil is absorbed.

2Butter the bread

Spread Kerrygold Spreadable Olive Oil evenly across all 3 slices of seeded wholemeal bread. For best results, eat bread at or after the bacon — eating protein and fat before carbs measurably flattens the glucose curve.

3Assemble

Layer the bacon across the bread and add Heinz Tomato Ketchup Zero to taste.

4Serve immediately

Plate and serve straight away while the bacon is hot and the bread is fresh.

Notes & Substitutions

  • Bread — Seeded wholemeal is key. Avoid commercial "sourdough" from supermarkets unless it is a genuine long-fermented bakery loaf. The bread choice accounts for the majority of the insulin impact in this meal.
  • Butter — Kerrygold Spreadable Olive Oil (60% butter, 26% olive oil) is the recommended choice. Avoid Kerrygold Spreadable (rapeseed oil blend). The pure Kerrygold block butter is also excellent.
  • Ketchup — Heinz Tomato Ketchup Zero contains no sugar. The spirit vinegar in it also has a mild benefit for insulin sensitivity. Alternatives: Heinz No Added Sugar (stevia-sweetened), Hunter & Gather Unsweetened Ketchup, mustard, or hot sauce.
  • Bacon — Back bacon is used here. Streaky bacon adds approximately 20 extra calories per rasher due to higher fat content.
