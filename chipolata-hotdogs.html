Chipolata
Hotdogs

High-protein, low-sugar hotdogs built for insulin sensitivity. Wholemeal rolls, 93% pork chipolatas, toasted in the sausage fat — no butter needed.

~576 kcal

Ingredients

IngredientAmountCalories
Sainsbury's TTD British Pork Chipolatas (93% pork)4 chipolatas265 kcal
Aldi Village Bakery Wholemeal Rolls2 rolls296 kcal
Extra virgin olive oil (for frying)½ tsp (~2.5g)20 kcal
Mustard (Dijon, English or American)~10g10 kcal
Heinz Tomato Ketchup Zero~15g5 kcal

Method

1Cook the chipolatas

Heat a pan to medium (level 4 out of 6 on an electric hob). Add ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil and cook the 4 chipolatas, turning occasionally, for 12–15 minutes until cooked through with no pink inside and lightly browned on all sides.

2Rest the sausages

Remove the chipolatas from the pan and set aside on a warm plate. Do not wipe the pan — the rendered sausage fat left behind is what you'll use to toast the rolls. No extra oil needed.

3Toast the rolls

Split the 2 wholemeal rolls in half and place cut-side down in the same pan over medium heat. Press down lightly. Toast for 1–2 minutes until golden and lightly crisped. The sausage fat gives the rolls a rich flavour without any added butter.

4Assemble and serve

Place 2 chipolatas in each toasted roll. Add mustard and Heinz Ketchup Zero to taste. Serve immediately — eat the sausages first before the bread where possible to further flatten the glucose curve.

Notes & Substitutions

  • Sausages — Sainsbury's TTD British Pork Chipolatas are 93% pork, RSPCA assured and outdoor bred — the cleanest chipolata available in a mainstream supermarket. Cook from chilled for best results.
  • Rolls — Aldi Village Bakery Wholemeal Rolls (6 pack, ~45p) are the recommended pick — genuine wholemeal, widely available, and sized right for 2 chipolatas per roll.
  • Toasting tip — The rendered fat from high-quality 93% pork chipolatas is clean and flavourful. Toasting the rolls in it replaces the need for butter entirely.
  • Mustard — Any plain mustard works: Dijon, English or American yellow. All are essentially sugar-free.
  • Ketchup — Heinz Tomato Ketchup Zero contains no sugar. See the Bacon Sandwich recipe for a full list of alternatives.
