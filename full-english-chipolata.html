Chipolata

A full English rebuilt for insulin sensitivity — high protein, healthy fats, no bread, no black pudding, no mushrooms or tomatoes. Every ingredient chosen to keep your glucose curve flat.

~634 kcal

Ingredients

IngredientAmountCalories
Back bacon rashers2 rashers130 kcal
Sainsbury's TTD Pork Chipolatas (93% pork)4 chipolatas264 kcal
Avocado, halved and stoned½ avocado115 kcal
Heinz No Added Sugar Baked Beans150g105 kcal
Extra virgin olive oil (for frying)½ tsp (~2.5g)20 kcal
Black pepper and chilli flakes (optional)1 pinch—

Method

1Start the chipolatas

Heat a pan to medium (level 4 out of 6 on an electric hob). Add ½ teaspoon extra virgin olive oil and cook the 4 chipolatas, turning occasionally, for 12–15 minutes until cooked through with no pink inside and lightly browned on all sides.

2Add the bacon

In the same pan, add the 2 bacon rashers. The bacon fat will render into the pan naturally alongside the chipolatas. Cook to your preference, around 4–5 minutes, turning once. No extra oil needed at this stage.

3Heat the beans

Warm the beans in a small saucepan over low heat, stirring occasionally. Do not boil. Season with black pepper and chilli flakes if using.

4Prepare the avocado

Slice or fan out the avocado. Season with black pepper and a small squeeze of lemon if available. The avocado provides healthy monounsaturated fats, fibre and potassium — all supportive of insulin sensitivity.

5Plate and serve

Arrange everything on a warmed plate. Eat protein and fat first — chipolatas, bacon, avocado — before the beans, to further flatten the glucose curve. Serve immediately.

Notes & Substitutions

  • Sausages — Sainsbury's TTD British Pork Chipolatas are 93% pork, RSPCA assured and outdoor bred. Aldi Specially Selected and Lidl Deluxe both offer 90% pork chipolatas as solid budget alternatives. Avoid any variety below approximately 88% pork.
  • Beans — Heinz No Added Sugar Baked Beans is the easiest option — 150g is roughly half a standard tin. Lidl Newgate and Aldi Bramwells No Added Sugar are solid budget alternatives. Always check the label.
  • Avocado — Provides monounsaturated fats, fibre and potassium, all of which support insulin sensitivity. Season with black pepper and a squeeze of lemon if available.
  • Bacon — Back bacon is used here. Streaky bacon adds approximately 20 extra calories per rasher due to higher fat content.
