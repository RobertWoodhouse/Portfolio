Tuna Pasta Salad
Avocado

A blood sugar-friendly tuna pasta salad — wholewheat pasta kept to a modest 60g portion to manage glycemic load, with avocado providing healthy fat and fibre in place of eggs. High protein, minimal sugar, no mayo.

~635 kcal

Ingredients

IngredientAmountCalories
Lidl Baresa 100% Durum Wholewheat Fusilli, dry weight60g205 kcal
Tuna in spring water, drained1 tin (~145g)130 kcal
Avocado, halved and stoned1 whole avocado230 kcal
Sweetcorn (no added sugar)50g40 kcal
Full-fat Greek yogurt2 tbsp30 kcal
Lemon, juiced1 lemon—
Black pepper1 pinch—

Method

1Cook the pasta

Cook the fusilli in a pan of boiling water according to packet instructions, usually around 10–12 minutes until al dente. Drain and rinse briefly under cold water to stop the cooking and cool slightly.

2Combine base ingredients

In a large bowl, combine the cooled pasta with the tuna and sweetcorn.

3Dress the salad

Stir through the Greek yogurt and squeeze in the juice of the lemon. Season with black pepper. Mix well to coat evenly.

4Add avocado and serve

Slice or dice the avocado and gently fold through the pasta, or arrange on top. Serve at room temperature or chilled — eat the tuna and avocado first where possible before the pasta to further flatten the glucose curve.

Notes & Substitutions

  • Avocado — A whole avocado is used here (not half) to bring the meal into the 550–650 kcal range now that the eggs and tomatoes are absent. For a lighter version, use half an avocado with a small extra portion of tuna — this brings it to around 545–560 kcal.
  • Pasta — 60g dry is the critical number — do not increase the pasta portion to add calories; use the avocado or extra tuna instead. Lidl Baresa 100% Durum Wholewheat Fusilli is the primary pick. Alternatives: Aldi Grower's Harvest Wholewheat Pasta or Sainsbury's Wholewheat Fusilli.
  • Tuna — Spring water is the correct choice: pure protein, no unnecessary calories from oil, no excess sodium from brine. Avoid tuna in sunflower oil entirely. Lidl own brand, Aldi own brand, John West, or Sainsbury's own all work well.
  • Sweetcorn — Check the label before buying. Some tinned sweetcorn contains added sugar in the brine. Look for "no added sugar" or "in water" varieties.
  • Greek yogurt — Full-fat Greek yogurt is the recommended mayo substitute — cleaner ingredients, higher protein. Hellmann's Light is an acceptable alternative if preferred (1 tbsp ≈ 35 kcal).
